World guide · Region

Central Bay Core

The San Francisco peninsula. The dense municipal heart of the commonwealth. The state's resilience bunkers are here, and so is its main hospital.

What is at stake here

If this region fails, the state loses its command and its hospital in the same hour. The Four Ledgers are read off a wall in the bunker here, and the patients those numbers describe are a few streets away.

Factions present

Joint Resilience TaskforceBay-State HealthThe Crimson Beacon

About the factions

The Cold Audit and Protocol Override begin here.

Locations

Eight Bridges Subterranean Bunker

A blast-shielded command center beneath the municipal core, lit by red emergency lights and amber telemetry. Its Command Bridge is where the Governor works; its Telemetry Vault holds the state's infrastructure data streams.

Bay-State Health ICU Command

A hyper-automated medical fortress run almost entirely by AI agent networks. The Main Triage Desk is where the Cold Audit begins. Behind it, a Power Backup Corridor where a skeleton staff rations battery power by hand.

The Tàikōng Consulate

A fortified compound near the western shore with a copper-shielded briefing room: the diplomatic bridge to the island that makes the state's chips.
