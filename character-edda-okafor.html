World guide · Character

Harbormaster Edda Okafor

Harbormaster, Eight Bridges Maritime Terminal

A veteran who holds the terminal together with skeleton crews, manual overrides and a deep distrust of her own automation. Unsentimental, territorial, reliable. She decides what docks in Costanoa.

What is at stake

In The Iron Gate she can offer manual control of two cranes, or one honest answer about why her scheduler started lying. Not both, not in forty minutes.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
What docks in Costanoa, and manual control of the cranes.
Cannot see
Why her scheduler started lying. Her automation no longer explains itself.

Relationships

  • Consul Liang: his ship needs her quay.
  • Director Eva Vega: her chain of command.
