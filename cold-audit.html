The Cold Audit

An interactive scenario exercise. About ten minutes, three decisions, four endings. Use a mouse, a tap, or the Tab and Enter keys. There is no timer and nothing is recorded.

The scenario needs JavaScript. If it does not load, the same decisions are set out in the facilitator kit.

DRAFT Not approved for publication. Scenario design by David A. Schoeller-Diaz, ROSHAI. Costanoa and everyone in it are fictional. Images are AI-generated illustrations from the Costanoa world.

The Cold Audit is a scenario exercise. Its board is a discussion device, not a model or a forecast.
