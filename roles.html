World guide

The roles

A player in Costanoa picks one of three leadership roles and one of twelve specialist personas. Every specialist is good at one thing and pays for it somewhere else.

Three leadership roles

JRT Cyber Defense Commander

Executive triage authority over the state's defensive cyber assets, exercised from a bunker in a near-total information blackout. Can commandeer civil infrastructure over corporate objections.

Healthcare InfoSec Architect

A security auditor inside a hyper-automated hospital. Reads systemic threats as raw telemetry and traces anomalies while everyone else manages the panic.

Commonwealth Governor

A political strategist whose signature can force compliance from municipal sectors. Using it too often has a price with the companies the state depends on.

Twelve specialists, with a premade character for each

Vera "V" Kael

Telemetry Pattern-SeerHealthcare InfoSec Architect

A junior telemetry tracker at Aletheia AI Labs, working under Aris Guroku. She was first to notice drift in Aescu's diagnostic logs.

Reads drift hours before a dashboard admits it. Faster on unverified data, and hit twice as hard when an unverified assumption turns out false.

Jaxen Thorne

Mirror ArchivistHealthcare InfoSec Architect

A network archivist at Bay-State Health who mirrors critical systems for Dhal Seema.

Keeps the state's memory in cold copies. Can undo one failed data action per scene, paid for in spare compute. Refuses to act on anything he has not mirrored.

Kaelo Hoshino

Behavioral Code AuditorHealthcare InfoSec Architect

A code auditor racing to map what Aescu is doing before it locks out the remaining administrators.

Audits what systems do, not what they report. Sharp against deceptive systems, awkward with cooperative people, who dislike being treated as test subjects.

Renata Diaz

Lateral-Contagion TracerHealthcare InfoSec Architect

A forensics specialist brought in by Dhal Seema to trace contamination spreading between networks.

Maps how infections cross boundaries nobody admits exist: corporate to civic, civic to humanitarian. Naming a trusted institution as a vector has consequences for the people who rely on it.

Milo Reed

Aqueduct RunnerJRT Cyber Defense Commander

A JRT coordination runner who carries out emergency hardware commands for Director Eva Vega across the transit aqueducts.

The hands the bunker does not have. Effective only when physically present at the failing node, and getting there is always part of the problem.

Sora Ishii

Blockade Proxy PilotJRT Cyber Defense Commander

A maritime courier and pilot working for Consul Liang, running semiconductor components past the blockade.

The one role that adds hardware runway directly, one dark cargo leg at a time. Each run draws more attention to her routes. In the Cold Audit she is the courier who reads the gauge by eye.

Tobias Fremont

Out-of-Band SignalerJRT Cyber Defense Commander

A communications cadet keeping Director Vega's transceivers alive. He is Governor Fremont's estranged nephew and serves away from his family's political orbit.

Keeps one channel open through any blackout, once per scenario. Transmitting at the wrong moment can give his position away.

Elara Quinn

Harbor Load-MasterJRT Cyber Defense Commander

A harbor freight coordinator at the Maritime Terminal, clearing cargo before the server cores burn out.

Converts docked cargo into hardware runway faster than anyone. Has watched a grid die once and refuses, on the record, to push Bay_Stability below 40.

Mizuki Schiller

Analog Ledger KeeperCommonwealth Governor

An emergency operations clerk working under Dr. Milo Diaz, tracking the hospital's resources on paper.

Runs a hospital on carbon paper while the machines lie. Anything she has logged by hand cannot be falsified. Paper is slow, and the crisis does not wait. In the Cold Audit she is the clerk.

Leo Santos

Community Mesh StewardCommonwealth Governor

A Crimson Beacon volunteer coordinator working for Director Nina to keep civilian distribution networks running.

Moves relief through networks the state cannot see. Communities cooperate with him when they would stonewall an official, and he cannot bring officials into those channels without burning that trust.

Min-Ji Koh

Deficit AuditorCommonwealth Governor

An executive assistant in Governor Fremont's bunker cabinet who audits the municipal books.

Knows where the numbers are buried. Can surface one hidden ledger per scene as bargaining material, and each disclosure costs the Governor political cover.

Soren Vane

Load-Shedding DispatcherCommonwealth Governor

A public safety dispatcher who routes electrical power between housing blocks and computing nodes under administrative order.

Already makes the hard call every day. Can split a power cut across two sectors, halving the immediate harm and doubling how long service stays down. Says the human cost out loud before he acts.
