World guide · Region

North-Central Valley

Marin and Sonoma, up to the border. The northern perimeter, from the Marin Headlands into the Sonoma agricultural technology sectors, and the hard edge with the U.S. Federation.

What is at stake here

This is where the Federation's pressure is physical. Convoys wait here, signals die here, and any deal the Governor makes with President Sterling's government would be enforced here first.

Factions present

Joint Resilience Taskforce

About the factions

Locations

Diablo Range Border Checkpoint

A fortified concrete barrier between Costanoa and U.S. Federation forces. Floodlights, armed guards and stranded civilian convoys on the Costanoan side; federal jamming arrays on the other.

Who you meet here

No scripted character is stationed here when the story opens. Players arrive from elsewhere, usually in a hurry.
