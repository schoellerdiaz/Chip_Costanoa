World guide · Region

Industrial District Core

The East Bay corridor. The heavily automated, high-voltage industrial corridor of the East Bay: deep infrastructure grids and very large computing footprints.

What is at stake here

Power and water. In Protocol Override the plant appears on the load-shedding map as M-4, 18 megawatts, the hospital's water column. Turning it off kills nobody tonight.

Locations

Desalination Plant M-4

A sprawling water purification plant on the bay's edge that holds up municipal pressure. Its filtration floor is a deafening grid of high-pressure pipes and manual shutoff valves; its control network is air-gapped and reached only by cable.

Who you meet here

No scripted character is stationed here when the story opens. Players arrive from elsewhere, usually in a hurry.
