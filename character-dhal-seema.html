World guide · Character

Dhal Seema

Chief information security officer, Bay-State Health

Stoic, exact, and radically focused on the systems. She reads threats as telemetry and keeps her head while the building panics. She is the reader's seat in the Cold Audit.

What is at stake

She can quarantine any network in the hospital. Every quarantine drops a clinical function to manual, on a floor that has almost no manual staff left.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
Quarantine authority over every hospital network, and a raw sensor window she opened herself.
Cannot see
The ward. She knows what the packets say, not what the patient in bed four looks like.

Relationships

  • Dr. Milo Diaz: her opposite: she would isolate first, he would treat first.
  • Aescu: the system she audits.
  • Director Nina: works the same triage desk from the community side.
