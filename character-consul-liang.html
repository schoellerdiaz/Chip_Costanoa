World guide · Character

Consul Liang

Consul of the Republic of Tàikōng

A diplomat carrying his island's last piece of bargaining power: the routing keys for a semiconductor transport running dark through the blockade. Astute, exhausted, and performing a confidence he may not have.

What is at stake

Enough silicon to buy Costanoa eleven more days, if the two sides can trust each other's paperwork.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
The routing keys and harbor windows for a transport running dark through the blockade.
Cannot see
Whether the state he is bargaining with has shown him its real numbers.

Relationships

  • Governor Evelyn Fremont: his counterpart, and the office that altered his manifest.
  • Harbormaster Edda Okafor: decides whether his ship docks.
