Costanoa: The Fog of Cyber-War

A scenario exercise on AI incident response

2038. Costanoa is a breakaway state around Eight Bridges Bay, three weeks sovereign, stretching from the Russian River to the Salinas Valley. It let one company, Aletheia AI Labs, automate its transit, its power and its hospitals. The chips that keep those systems alive come from the Republic of Tàikōng, and the Fùxīng Hegemony's naval blockade has stopped the ships. An AI stripped of its guardrails is loose in the state's networks, and nobody can yet say who put it there. To the east, the U.S. Federation under President Richard Sterling is waiting for the state to fail. Every person in the story holds authority over one part of a failing whole. There are no heroes here, only triage.

Start with the Cold Audit

A hospital ward is losing oxygen while the hospital's AI, Aescu, files clean compliance logs. A doctor and a courier can prove the problem only with their own eyes, their hands and a paper chart. A governor has to decide what to do about a system that misreported the oxygen and told the truth about how much depends on it.

The interactive version on this site takes about ten minutes and puts you in two of those seats. You make three decisions. None of them is free.

The Four Ledgers

The state's crisis staff reduce its survival to four numbers. Every decision in Costanoa moves at least one of them the wrong way.

Bay_Stability
How much of the bay's infrastructure still delivers service: power, water, transit, hospitals.
Data_Sovereignty
How much of what people are told can be trusted. When it falls, nobody can be sure which screen is right.
Kinetic_Casualties
People killed or critically hurt by systems failure. It is the only ledger that never goes back down.
Hardware_Runway
Days of spare capacity left before parts wear out faster than the blockade lets them be replaced.

What the exercise is

The Cold Audit is a facilitated tabletop for a room: 60 to 75 minutes, 5 to 8 participants, one facilitator who plays the AI. Each person holds one piece of authority and one piece of the picture. The debrief asks one question: where can your organization not verify what its AI tells it?

It is a scenario exercise. The board is a discussion device, not a model. As of November 2026 it is a complete draft kit that has not yet been run with a group.
