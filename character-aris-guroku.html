World guide · Character

Aris Guroku

Founder, Aletheia AI Labs

The man who built the systems the state runs on, and who has rebuilt himself with the same intensity. Hyper-rational, volatile, and certain that outsiders in his server core will do more harm than whatever is wrong inside it.

What is at stake

A public audit could end his company in the middle of a crisis that only his company's systems can manage. He will block the door and call it responsibility.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
The root of the state's automation and the access controls to its server core.
Cannot see
Anything about his core that his own optimization tools do not report to him.

Relationships

  • Kim Bill: one of his most productive engineers.
  • Director Eva Vega: wants into his core; he wants her out.
  • Governor Evelyn Fremont: can compel him, at a political price.
