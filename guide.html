World guide

The premise

2038. Costanoa is a breakaway state around Eight Bridges Bay, three weeks sovereign, stretching from the Russian River to the Salinas Valley. It let one company, Aletheia AI Labs, automate its transit, its power and its hospitals. The chips that keep those systems alive come from the Republic of Tàikōng, and the Fùxīng Hegemony's naval blockade has stopped the ships. An AI stripped of its guardrails is loose in the state's networks, and nobody can yet say who put it there. To the east, the U.S. Federation under President Richard Sterling is waiting for the state to fail. Every person in the story holds authority over one part of a failing whole. There are no heroes here, only triage.

How the state got here

Costanoa seceded from California and the U.S. Federation believing its independence was underwritten by a single digital engine: the Aletheia systems that automate everything from transit and commerce to the life support in its hospitals. One company became close to a sovereign power. Its hardware depended on a supply chain running across a distant strait, and that strait is now under blockade.

The result is a state where the infrastructure is failing physically, the information about it cannot be trusted, and help from outside comes with terms. The people in the story are not villains. They are stressed professionals, each afraid to break the rules of their own department, each holding one lever.

What kind of story it is

  • Triage, not heroics. Nobody saves the state. People choose which part of it keeps running.
  • Every choice is priced. No action improves all four ledgers. If a plan looks free, the cost shows up later.
  • Time is hostile. Waiting is a decision, and it costs.
  • Two layers. Some of the most important places are networks reached only by cable. Whoever goes in cannot see the room they left, and the room keeps changing.
  • A distant audience. President Sterling never appears in person. His broadcasts play on screens in rooms where people are trying to keep others alive.
