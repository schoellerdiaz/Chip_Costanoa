World guide

The six regions

Costanoa runs from the Russian River to the Salinas Valley, walled off from the U.S. Federation by the Diablo Range. Six regions, each with something the others cannot do without.

Central Bay Core

The San Francisco peninsula

The dense municipal heart of the commonwealth. The state's resilience bunkers are here, and so is its main hospital.

Industrial District Core

The East Bay corridor

The heavily automated, high-voltage industrial corridor of the East Bay: deep infrastructure grids and very large computing footprints.

North-Central Valley

Marin and Sonoma, up to the border

The northern perimeter, from the Marin Headlands into the Sonoma agricultural technology sectors, and the hard edge with the U.S. Federation.

Harbor Blockade Perimeter

The Pacific approach

Open ocean outside the Golden Gate strait, patrolled by blockade fleets, and the last deep-water terminal the state still runs.

Inland Governance Seat

Sacramento and the river confluence

The high-security political interior around the river confluences, where the capital's administrative machinery sits behind river patrols.

South Bay Cyber-Core

Research and hardware country

The sprawling research and development perimeter: hardware design yards and the codebase nodes the rest of the state runs on.
