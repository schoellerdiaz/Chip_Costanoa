World guide · Character

Director Nina

Leader of the Crimson Beacon

Empathetic, resilient and badly overstretched. She keeps clinics and supply lines moving for the people the official systems reach last, and she has friends on every hospital floor.

What is at stake

She can bring experienced hands into a hospital that laid them off. Nobody has vetted the channels she would use.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
The trust of communities the official systems reach last, and a way to bring laid-off clinicians back into the building.
Cannot see
How the low-cost software her network runs on actually works, or what else it connects to.

Relationships

  • Dr. Milo Diaz: an ally on the clinical floor.
  • Dhal Seema: sees her network as a risk as well as a resource.
