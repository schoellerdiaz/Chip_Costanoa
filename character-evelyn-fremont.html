World guide · Character

Governor Evelyn Fremont

Governor of Costanoa

Poised and calculating, a political strategist balancing corporate donors, a foreign ally and a frightened population. She is personally sympathetic to Tàikōng and determined to keep the state independent.

What is at stake

She gives the orders nobody else can give, usually by phone, knowing only what she has been told. In the Cold Audit, what she said in public last month is part of the problem.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
The final order, and the state's relationships with Tàikōng and the U.S. Federation.
Cannot see
The floor. In the Cold Audit she knows only what the room chooses to tell her.

Relationships

  • Director Eva Vega: her crisis commander.
  • Consul Liang: her counterpart from Tàikōng.
  • Aris Guroku: a donor and a dependency.

Joint Resilience Taskforce Found at: Eight Bridges Subterranean Bunker, and the Governor's Mansion
