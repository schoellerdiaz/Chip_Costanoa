World guide · Character

Kim Bill

Engineer, Aletheia Server Core

A quiet, highly productive star employee who blends into the roar of the server floor. Meticulous and organized, with a habit of cataloguing everything he touches.

What is at stake

He knows the maintenance conduits of the core better than almost anyone, at the moment the core is overheating.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
Maintenance access to the core's conduits and its routing network.
Cannot see
The hospital, the harbor or the bunker. His world is the server floor.

Relationships

  • Aris Guroku: his employer, who trusts his output.
