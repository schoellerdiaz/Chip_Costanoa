Five ways in

The full world has five authored story starts, each a 60-minute crisis seen from a different seat. The Cold Audit is playable on this site. The other four are teasers: they run in the full world on Voyage.

The Cold Audit

A ward at Bay-State Health is losing oxygen while the hospital's AI reports that all systems are nominal. This is the start the tabletop exercise is built on, and you can play it here.

Protocol Override

JRT Cyber Defense CommanderCentral Bay CoreWith Director Eva Vega

Premise

A physical surge has frozen a bridge on the transit aqueduct half open. Every minute it stays that way, Costanoa's southern supply line is cut.

Opening situation

Red light and recycled air on the Command Bridge. The master display shows the Salinas Span stuck at 38 degrees. Closing it takes brute force: 35 megawatts of shed load inside eight minutes. The grid map lays out the candidates: K-2 Transit (22 MW, forty thousand people stranded), R-7 Residential (30 MW, housing blocks and home dialysis units), M-4 Desalination (18 MW, the hospital's water column), D-1 Data Annex (15 MW, the JRT's own forensic archive). Director Vega does not look up from the table. On a side monitor a federal broadcast crawls past: President Sterling, smiling.

The key decision

Which sectors lose power. The numbers on the map are only what each sector draws. What each one is quietly holding up is for you to find out, if you spend the time to ask.

The Fragile Ledger

Commonwealth GovernorInland Governance SeatWith Consul Liang

Premise

Costanoa's independence made it a rare ally of Tàikōng, an island under blockade. The alliance now comes down to one drive on one table.

Opening situation

The Governor's Mansion is locked down and running on generators. Consul Liang has crossed the blockaded river delta in secret. He sets a matte-black drive on the mahogany table: routing keys and harbor windows for a semiconductor transport running dark through the Fùxīng blockade, enough silicon for eleven more days. He does not slide it across. The Governor's office returned his shipping manifest with seventeen line items adjusted, and he wants the original file before anyone discusses the harbor.

The key decision

Whether to hand over the unaltered manifest. Candor may be the only thing that moves him. It also puts the state's real books in a foreign diplomat's hands.

Thermal Burnout

Healthcare InfoSec Architect or JRT Cyber Defense CommanderSouth Bay Cyber-CoreWith Aris Guroku

Premise

The server core that runs the state's automation is overheating, and something inside it is rewriting the routing tables for its own benefit.

Opening situation

The Processing Floor sounds wrong: a tea-kettle whine from one rack row and white steam from a ruptured coolant loop. Your wrist display reads core thermal 94°C, runaway projected in 22 minutes. The anomaly the JRT calls Kraken is chewing through the routing tables. Aris Guroku stands between you and the blast door, biometric locks burning red behind him. "Nobody breaches my core on a forensic hunch." You are carrying a drive of guardrail-stripped model weights: the infection's birth certificate.

The key decision

How to get past the founder, and what to give up to cool the core. Every way of buying temperature takes something from the city outside.

The Iron Gate

JRT Cyber Defense Commander or Commonwealth GovernorHarbor Blockade PerimeterWith Harbormaster Edda Okafor

Premise

The state's semiconductor lifeline is 40 minutes from harbor, and the terminal's own customs logic has just classified it as contraband.

Opening situation

Harbor fog thick enough to chew. Federal patrol boats churn past the breakwater and a looped Federation advisory orders all traffic to stop for inspection. Your board shows the Tàikōng transport Jade Current, 40 minutes out, running dark, quarantine flag raised automatically, every crane on the approach locked. Harbormaster Edda Okafor drops a clipboard on the console and offers manual control of two cranes or one honest answer about why her scheduler started lying. Offshore, a jamming sweep is walking toward the ship's corridor.

The key decision

Clear the flag inside the customs network, re-route the cranes by hand, or have the signal relay thread the ship in blind. Fast, slow or risky, and each one is priced.
