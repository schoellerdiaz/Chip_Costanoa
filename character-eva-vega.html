World guide · Character

Director Eva Vega

Commander, Joint Resilience Taskforce

Fierce, direct, and practical to the point of harshness. She runs the state's crisis response from a vault under the city, with authority to commandeer civil infrastructure and almost no trustworthy picture of it.

What is at stake

Every order she gives turns something off for someone. She has decided that not choosing is worse.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
Authority to commandeer civil infrastructure, and the wall where the Four Ledgers are read.
Cannot see
Past the information blackout. Most of what reaches her has a single source.

Relationships

  • Governor Evelyn Fremont: the Governor she answers to.
  • Aris Guroku: the vendor whose systems she must override.
  • Harbormaster Edda Okafor: holds the harbor for her.
