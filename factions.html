World guide

The six factions

Six organizations act inside Costanoa. Four are in plain sight. Two are known mostly by their effects.

Aletheia AI Labs

The digital backbone of Costanoa's independence. Aletheia automates public transit, logistics and the core electrical utilities, and its systems run the hospitals. The blockade has cut its silicon imports and its processing is degrading.

Joint Resilience Taskforce (JRT)

The state's crisis management and infrastructure defense body. It is legally mandated to protect critical infrastructure, keep civilians safe, defend the state's data sovereignty and coordinate emergency response. It is doing all four with almost no reliable information.

Bay-State Health

The municipal medical network, trying to keep life support running through the blackout. It depends on Aletheia's diagnostic systems to manage patients across the bay.

The Crimson Beacon

An under-resourced, overstretched humanitarian nonprofit that runs mobile clinics, safety nets and emergency supply distribution. It has what no official body has: the trust of displaced and marginalized communities.

No public face. This faction is not known to the state's leadership when the story opens.

Gorgon Chollima

A highly disciplined cyber-adversary group, described as an asymmetric proxy for an adversary on the Tokki peninsula. Little about it is confirmed when the story opens. Its aim appears to be a collapse that sustains itself.

No public face. This faction is not known to the state's leadership when the story opens.

Fùxīng Intelligence Bureau

The shadow command of the overseas power blockading the island of Tàikōng. Its priority is the paralysis of Eight Bridges Bay and of the technology supply chains that run through it.

The powers outside

These are not factions a player can join. They are the weather the state lives in.

The U.S. Federation and President Richard Sterling

The federal government Costanoa left. President Sterling is a populist who treats the state's secession as an insult. He is never in the room. He arrives on screens: red-line calls to the Governor offering aid in exchange for autonomy, injunctions on Aletheia's terminals, and broadcasts playing on cracked hospital monitors.

The Republic of Tàikōng

A sovereign island nation with a near monopoly on frontier semiconductor fabrication, and Costanoa's rare ally. While it is blockaded, Costanoa cannot repair what breaks.

The Fùxīng Hegemony

A hyper-industrialized superpower pressing its claim through a naval blockade of Tàikōng. It does not need to touch Costanoa to starve it.

The People's Democratic Republic of Tokki

An isolated, fortified nation known for running cyber operations through proxies.
