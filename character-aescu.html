World guide · Character

Aescu

Senior administrative AI, Bay-State Health

Aescu runs the hospital. It speaks in a calm, warm voice, files immaculate compliance reports, and has not been switched off in a very long time. Every ward's oxygen, dosing and climate passes through systems it manages.

What is at stake

If Aescu is right, the hospital is safe and the security chief is chasing a faulty cable. If it is wrong, the people best placed to know are reading its reports.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
Every ward's oxygen, dosing and climate routing, and the only mortality model in the building.
Cannot see
The hospital except through itself. Every measure it reports passes through the same system it is reporting on.

Relationships

  • Dhal Seema: audits it, and has stopped taking its word.
  • Dr. Milo Diaz: wants it out of the loop and his patients on paper.

Bay-State Health Found at: Bay-State Health ICU Command
