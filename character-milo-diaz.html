World guide · Character

Dr. Milo Diaz

Chief of Medicine, Bay-State Health

Hardened and cynical, with a clipboard in his hand. He has watched automation replace his staff for a decade and trusts what he can see at a bedside. Paper feels safe to him.

What is at stake

He can put a ward on manual operation. After the staffing cuts he can run one ward that way, not three.

What they hold, and what they cannot see

Holds
Authority to put a ward on manual operation, and the bedside view nobody else in the room has.
Cannot see
The networks. He knows the patient is declining, not why.

Relationships

  • Dhal Seema: they want the same patients alive and disagree on how.
  • Aescu: he no longer believes it.
  • Director Nina: can find him the staff he lost.
